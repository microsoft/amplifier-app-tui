## Approved direction

The steward accepted the Ratatui-versus-OpenTUI comparison and explicitly asked to update
this project's vision/contracts using the supplied Converge method before deriving work.
They require the experience to feel at least as fast as their current CLI and permit
replacing parts as needed while generally supporting bundles, modules and swappable parts.

Consequences now reflected in our direction:

- Stop investing in Textual as the target frontend. Keep the existing slice as a
  reference/test harness while comparing the two candidates in real terminals.
- Require visual fidelity and clear review questions, not just functional test results.
- Do not freeze Python, PreparedBundle, the current host, or a sidecar process as law.
  Preserve thin-kernel responsibilities and independently useful ecosystem seams.
- Measure the complete experience against a matched CLI baseline. A fast renderer,
  a language choice, or the phrase "small process boundary" is not evidence.

## What does not change

The product is still an Amplifier terminal experience with Work/Review/System, persistent
intent, attributable evidence and modular composition. We are not building the future
Converge service or replacing this product's views with that app's Direction/Operation.
No upstream source is changed in this amendment. Existing runtime work is retained.
